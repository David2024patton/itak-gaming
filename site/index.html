What it is

iTaK Gaming turns a cheap streaming stick or a spare PC into a full retro arcade for your TV. It was built by a disabled Army veteran for his own family, then open sourced so your family can use it too. The emulators are our own code, written from scratch in Go. No RetroArch dependency, nothing phoning home.

Why families like it

Per-player save states

Every player gets their own profile and 10 save slots per game. Dad's progress never overwrites the kids', and nobody loses their game.

Play together online

A public lobby at arcade.itak.live pairs players across the internet, with a waiting room, ready check, and countdown. Kentucky vs Missouri, same game, zero desyncs in testing.

Made for the couch

TV remote, Bluetooth remote, gamepad, or keyboard. Big text, simple menus, works on a Chromecast HD and other modest hardware.

Our own emulator cores

Nine systems run on cores we wrote ourselves in Go, verified with test suites. That also means a clean open source license story.

Self-updating

The TV app checks for new builds, downloads them with checksum verification, and installs through the system installer. Ship it to family and it stays current.

Yours to run

MIT licensed code. Clone the repo, build for your platform, and run your own arcade. No store, no subscription, no lock-in.

About games

iTaK Gaming ships with no games and no ROMs, and it never will. Use your own legally obtained backups of games you own. The code is open source; your game library stays your business, on your hardware.
