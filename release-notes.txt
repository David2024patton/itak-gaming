Sound fix, second pass. The first pass fixed the sound rate, but on heavy screens the app's sound cushion was still swinging between off and full blast every few seconds, and that swinging bends the pitch of the music. The cushion now measures how far behind a screen actually is and holds exactly that much correction, steady, so the music stays at pitch. It still guards against dropouts on the heaviest screens, and games that run at full speed stay on the exact, untouched sound path. Games, saves, and settings carry over untouched.
