Renaming a player can no longer strand their play history. Before, if a fresh stats file existed under the new name, the rename quietly skipped the move and the player's time and launches stayed behind under the old name, which is how a certain Missouri player's history detached last week. A rename now merges the two histories game by game: launches and seconds add up, and the file takes the new name. The app also checks on every launch for a stats file wearing a stale name label and quietly re-labels it, so the Leaderboard and Manage players show the right totals right away. Saved games are untouched. Everything else from 1.110 carries over.
