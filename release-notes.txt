The host runs the room now: the waiting room shows everyone in the lobby (Seat 1 host, Seat 2, and who is Watching). The host can tap a watcher to move them into Seat 2; the old Seat 2 rotates out to the watcher seats on their own screen, no one has to leave the lobby. During a game the pause menu has End match for the host and an I Lost button for both players; either one ends the match and drops everyone back into the waiting room, ready for the next game on the same connection.
