Sound fix for SNES and GBA games. The stick was converting their sound to the TV rate on its own, and that conversion could turn harsh and scratchy, like the noise on Zelda's file select screen. The app now does that conversion itself, carefully, so the sound that reaches your TV is the sound the game made. The Sound test screen also gained a game sound path test that plays the same path your games use, so sound can be checked there too. Nothing else changed. Your games, saves, and settings carry over untouched.
