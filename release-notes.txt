Audio synchronization and resampling fixes across all emulator cores. First, on Android hardware where the audio output operates at 48,000 Hz, in-app sinc resampling is now activated for all cores whose output rate differs from native (including 44,100 Hz cores such as NES, Genesis, Game Boy, and PS1). This eliminates platform mixer jitter, audio dropouts, and enables low-latency fast-track playback. Second, in the SNES core, APU communication ports $2140 to $2143 now synchronize master clock cycles with the SPC700 sub-CPU prior to port reads and writes, matching jgenesis and snes-apu timing to eliminate handshake races during audio driver boot and command transfers.
