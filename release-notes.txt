Sound fix, aimed at the scratchy, screechy sound some players have heard on SNES games. The app was treating the SNES sound chip as running at exactly 32,000 Hz. The real chip runs closer to 32,040, and that small mismatch made the sound slowly drift until the correction popped. The app now runs the sound at the measured rate and fine tunes the conversion continuously, the way the big emulators do, instead of stretching the sound in a separate step. The sound test in Settings plays through the same corrected path. Games, saves, and settings carry over untouched.
