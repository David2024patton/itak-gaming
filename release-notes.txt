Sound rebuilt to match the working emulators. The SNES sound chip now runs at its real measured speed inside the core itself, the game now waits on the sound buffer the way the proven emulators do instead of pushing ahead on heavy screens, and the sound report now counts the one place samples could be dropped. The Picture and Display settings are gone: Sharp picture and full-speed drawing are built in, so nothing can conflict with the sound. Player screen, remote, and controller fixes all carry over. Games and saves carry over untouched.
