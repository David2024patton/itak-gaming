Game Boy Advance now runs on our own core too, written in Go from the published hardware documentation like Genesis, Game Boy, NES and Super Nintendo: the full ARM7TDMI processor, every video mode, complete sound, save states, and battery saves in SRAM, Flash and EEPROM, all proven against the open-source homebrew game Anguna. mGBA is retired from the Game Boy Advance slot. Sega CD support stays on Genesis Plus GX as before; its replacement is in progress.
