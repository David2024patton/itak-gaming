A sound fix for the SNES engine. The sound chip's final mix was reading two of its four volume registers swapped, and folding the echo into the main sound before scaling instead of scaling each on its own the way the hardware does. On screens with strong echo, worst of all Zelda's player select, the left channel's echo played almost twice as loud as it should while the right channel played far too quiet, which is the loud shrill wash reported there. The mix now follows the hardware exactly: each channel's dry sound is scaled by its own master volume and its echo by its own echo volume. Measured on the bench, the player select music's two channels now balance (they were nearly six to one before), and nothing about the music itself changed. Games, saves, and play stats are untouched. Everything else from 1.111 carries over.
