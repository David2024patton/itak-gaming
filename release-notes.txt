Sega CD gets its own entry in the system grid, running on the same Go Genesis core as cartridges: the sub processor and CD drive, the graphics scaler, CD audio and the PCM chip, the BIOS service layer and backup RAM, all built from the published manuals and proven on a synthetic test disc that exercises all seven stages. Genesis Plus GX is retired, which closes the switchover: every system in the app now runs on our own emulator code, written here from the hardware documentation. Next up is the 64-bit lineup: Nintendo 64, Nintendo DS, PSP, Dreamcast, Saturn and GameCube are all finished and in testing for the 64-bit build.
