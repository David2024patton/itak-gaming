A sound accuracy fix for the SNES engine. The sound chip's GAIN envelope linear decrease was stepping by 8 per tick from an old build spec; real hardware steps by 32, the mirror of linear increase. Sounds that fade with custom GAIN ramps (percussion, sound effects, note tails) were decaying four times too slowly and hanging in the mix. The step now matches the hardware in both the shipping core and the Go reference core, each pinned by a test that fails on the old value. The bench renders of Zelda's rain, bedroom, and doorway scenes are byte-identical before and after, so nothing about those songs changes. Also corrected: two sound test programs that wrote the echo volume register while calling it the right master volume; they now write the real register. Everything else from 1.112 carries over.
