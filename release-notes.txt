Player screens fixed for the TV remote, and the remote plays games again. In the picture picker you can now move down into the pictures and pick one with the remote; the pictures sit on light tiles so dark hair and dark features show up properly, and a picture that cannot load now shows a plain placeholder instead of spinning forever. In Add a player, Manage players, and the player picker, the row you are on now gets a bright green outline, so you can always see where the remote is. The TV remote also works as a controller in games again: the app was mistaking the remote for a gamepad and locking it out. Games, saves, and settings carry over untouched.
