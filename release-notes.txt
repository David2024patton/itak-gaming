Controller fix. If your controller stopped working after you paired it again, this is why, and it is fixed: the app used to remember your controller by a temporary number that Android changes every time a controller pairs, so after a re-pair the game held your seat for the old number and threw your button presses away. Controllers are now remembered by their permanent identity, so pairing again just works. And if your controller is not connected, any other controller can drive your seat for that session instead of locking you out. You do not need to set anything up: just pick up your controller and play. Games, saves, and settings carry over untouched.
