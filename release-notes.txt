Three fixes from the couch. One: the pause menu no longer makes the game lag and jitter on the way in and out. The sound stream used to stop dead while the menu was up, the TV's sound hardware gave up on it, and the restart churn was the hang. The stream now stays alive on silence while the menu is open, so coming back is smooth. Two: the pause menu has a Fast-forward switch that runs the game as fast as the box can go with the sound held, handy for blowing through long intros. Three: pressing BACK in the app now asks first, with a choice to exit to the TV home screen, restart the app, or stay. The debug stats panel also learned two new lines: the number of the frame on screen, and which buttons are being pressed, so a controller problem can be seen while it happens.
