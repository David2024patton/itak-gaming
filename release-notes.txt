Got Next is here, plus tournaments. In a watched room anyone can tap Got Next to join the line; when a match ends the winner stays and the head of the line takes seat 2 automatically, and the host gets a Random challenger button for a surprise pick. Naming your lobby as a tournament when you create it turns on the record book: a declared loss reports the match automatically, the host can crown a Champion from the room, and the Leaderboard gains a Tournament board with records, titles, and recent results, separate from the playtime boards.
